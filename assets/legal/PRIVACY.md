# Privacy Policy for Screw Out

**Policy Version: 1.0**  
**Effective Date: October 16, 2026**  
**Last Updated: October 16, 2026**

## Introduction

Screw Out ("we," "our," or "us") is a puzzle game published by Pranta Dutta. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Screw Out mobile app (the "App") collects, how it is used, stored and shared, and the choices you have.

Screw Out is built to work **offline and without an account**. You can play every bundled sheet as a guest; signing in is optional and only used to back up and restore your progress.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Information you give us (optional account)

If you choose to **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Apple**:
- Your email address, display name and the unique account ID provided by Google or Apple
- A Firebase Authentication user ID and sign-in tokens
- A username we generate for you (used on leaderboards)

If you never sign in, none of this is collected.

### 2. Game data

- Sheets (levels) cleared, best moves and times, mastery marks
- Daily sheet completions and streak
- Coin balance and a record of coins earned and spent
- Themes you own and the theme you have equipped
- Settings: sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, high refresh rate, daily reminder, language

This data is always stored on your device. If you sign in, it is also synced to our server so it can be restored on another device.

### 3. Purchase information

- Which products you bought (Pro subscription, Remove ads, coin packs, theme packs), purchase tokens and transaction IDs, subscription status and expiry
- Payment itself is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. **We never see or store your card or payment details.**

### 4. Device and usage information

- An installation ID created by the App (not linked to your identity unless you sign in)
- App version, platform (Android/iOS), device model and OS version
- Your time-zone offset, so a reminder arrives in your evening rather than at night
- Gameplay events used to keep puzzles fair: sheet started/cleared/restarted, moves, time, hints, undos, blocked taps, times the tray filled up, and use of an extra slot
- Session counts and length

We do **not** collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.

### 5. Notifications

- A Firebase Cloud Messaging token, if you allow notifications, so we can send the optional daily-sheet reminder

## How We Use Your Information

- **Run the game:** save progress, verify purchases, apply Pro and Remove-ads benefits, unlock themes
- **Sync and restore** your progress if you sign in
- **Leaderboards:** show your generated username with your cleared sheets, daily result and streak
- **Reminders:** at most one optional daily-sheet reminder, which you can turn off in Settings or your device settings
- **Improve fairness and difficulty:** aggregated level statistics (for example, how often a sheet is restarted) tell us which puzzles are too hard or confusing
- **Keep the service secure** and prevent fraud (for example, duplicate purchase claims)

We do not sell your personal information.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Screw Out shows ads through **Google AdMob**:
- **Rewarded videos** — always optional; you choose to watch one for an extra hint or an extra tray slot
- **Interstitial ads** — only between sheets, never during a puzzle, and not on the first sheets
- **Banner ads** — on menu screens only, never on the puzzle screen
- **App open ads** — occasionally, when you come back after a long break

**Pro** and **Remove ads** turn off interstitial, banner and app open ads.

Google and its partners may use device identifiers and your advertising ID to show and measure ads. In regions where it is required (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland) we ask for your consent using Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown; on iOS we ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you decline, you will see non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy choices**, and reset your advertising ID in your device settings. More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Data Storage, Security and Retention

- Data on your device stays there until you uninstall the App or delete your account.
- Data on our server is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS).
- **Guests:** anonymous data not linked to an account is kept only as aggregated statistics. Inactive anonymous records are pruned after 90 days.
- **Accounts:** kept while your account exists. Purchase records may be kept longer where required for tax, accounting or fraud prevention.

## Deleting Your Data

- In the App: **Settings → Account → Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account, synced progress and sign-in identity from our servers.
- Or email **prantadutta1997@gmail.com** from the address linked to your account and we will delete it within 30 days.
- Uninstalling the App removes all data stored on your device.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

## Children

Screw Out is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## International Transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.
